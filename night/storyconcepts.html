Story Ideas

This article features various ideas for books. With all the ideas below I have a lot more planned than is written here, please ask if any concept is of interest.

Super Power Auction

A company invents superpowers. Each person can only have one super power. Good super powers are very expensive while the cheaper ones are nearly free and are sold on an online auction site somewhat like eBay.

The story initial follows a small boy from a poor background. He is very much enamoured by the new site. He follows news media, blogs and talks to people trying to decide which power he should choose when he is the right age.

I envisage this as a mildly humorous short story although with other ideas I am sure this could be developed into a full novel.

Super powers did not show up through contact with an alien race, divine power or through a science experiment anomaly, they appeared in an infomercial. When it was discovered not to be a hoax the media went almost as crazy as shares in Lycra.

The strengths of the story are in my opinion that:

  • The main character shows character development.
  • The main character has a clear, believable intrinsic motivation.
  • The situation is simple to explain and has a low essential character count making it a good short story.
  • The best bits about most super hero related things are the bit where the characters discover their powers and make their moral choices (genesis), this focuses on that.
  • There is a lot of potential humour in the naming and potential stupid things people with newly found super powers would do. Especially if the protagonist follows social media as more ludicrous things are probable, if you assume you take the silliest things anyone in the world has done.

Downsides:

  • Potentially one main character and no support characters can be very dry although this is easy to fix.
  • Would be very hard to profit from as short, hard to categorise stories are not in high demand.
  • Readers may feel let down if the book ends just as the character gets their powers.

Inspiration: Listening to Soon I will be Invincible while trading on Diablo 3’s auction house.

Future Studies

I have a lot of different ideas falling under this category. The first is a serious book making the case for teaching about the future.  In my view there is an endemic problem with western society that people take a very short term view and often cling to outdated precepts; this is worst manifested in politics. A short section of the book is seen below:

Future

History is studied in most schools in the developed world, there are a multitude of serious books on the subject and there is much it can teach us. I propose a new subject Future. The future is unknown and even the best proponents will never be entirely right but does that mean it doesn’t deserve our thought or for repositories of knowledge to rival those of historic scholars?

If you want to study the future now, what would you do? For certain subjects there are already books available, economic and political text books on the future do exist, if rare, you can read about end of the world scenarios, but a lot of the information you can find is in science fiction. 

If you can model the future well, you are rich. If you can model the future well you can make huge political change. If you can model the future well you might even avert a disaster that could end humanity.

As a teacher I’ve taught hundreds of children from poorer backgrounds and one of the scariest things I noticed is, very few of them plan ahead or even think very hard about anything beyond their next party. Studying the future can help spot skill shortages for work opportunities, can teach planning or can help students draw likely conclusions from evidence.

Modelling the future and drawing conclusions from it makes money and a lot of money if you are right as it helps you to predict the companies and industries that will do well and therefore are best invested in. It can help you predict trends ahead of time a key skill in media and entrepreneurship of all kinds.

Near future investigation can help drive invention. If you know what technologies are available already and believe me, if you want to accurately predict the future you need to; it is not a big step to combine some, to create an idea worthy of being called an invention in its own right.

In this book I aim to describe how the subject Future would work, convince you that the claims already made are not marketing hype, explain how it would help you and draw some briefly thought through possibilities.

Principles of Studying the Future

How can possibly predict the future? Surely you are going to be wrong? Isn’t this just a waste of time?

These are some tough questions. You have to accept in future studies that you will not always be right! This sentence is important being one of the key principles of the subject. It is reasonable to predict that there will be an Olympic games in 2024, as Future studies based predictions go this is one of the safer ones but is this necessarily true? Many things could happen to prevent this, so many in fact that a comprehensive list is impossible to write. Despite a near infinite list of potential events that could prevent this, I still think this event is likely to happen. You should never bet everything on a single expected outcome from future studies however plausible it seems!

Strengths

  • Speculating on the future is interesting.
  • I believe the concept is actually correct, to fully explain why though, I would have to write most of the book.
  • I believe the book is stating an original point of view or at least an argument that hasn’t been debated and publicised.
  • The book has quite high potential to educate the audience.
  • There is a huge amount of information and if successful would be easy to write a full franchise.

Weaknesses

  • It will be hard to convince people that it is worthwhile trying to predict the future, partially because it is extremely hard to do quickly enough for a synopsis.
  • It would be hard to write the book without short term predictions, if they are wrong you lose credibility.
  • Honest predictions for UK and USA are likely to be slightly negative (this is most of your audience and they may not appreciate this).
  • Hard to categorise, it will be put into education sections and it probably wants to be in self-help.

The History of the 21st Century

This is similar to the previous concept but written as science fiction. The protagonist is in a university class in the future, studying the 21st century. I envisage a global job bank where a huge string of requirements are needed for all desirable jobs. A job appears with just a salary and list of requirements but no explanation for what the actual job is. Being ludicrously well paid the job generates a media buzz. The last requirement for several students is the above mentioned course. I am considering making the job involve time travel to the present day but this seems slightly cheesy.

Strengths

  • If your predictions were right you could plausibly end up with a lot of free publicity.
  • Education settings are good for explaining naturally.
  • Future class rooms and roleplaying style learning where students act as characters from history allows interesting snippets and for a dynamic story.
  • Education setting offers character development and motivation.
  • The mystery of the job is a hook for readers.
  • The future is still interesting.
  • There is less pressure to be right than in the previous concept.
  • It is also a lot less dry.
  • It is similarly easy to expand and make into a franchise.
  • Properly named it will easily be categorised as science fiction.

Weaknesses

  • Typical science fiction reader may not like this style of story as it is a departure from most common science fiction (This is minor as this is quite an adaptable audience).
  • Have to be wary of legal action from real people or businesses you may mention.

Murder Mystery Weekend

In my view a stately home hosted murder mystery weekend is a wonderful setting for an actual murder mystery. This is because the house can have a range of interesting things and plot devices set up. For example you could get the participants to hand in their mobile phone, host it on a remote island, have most people not realise the murder is actually real for a while after it happens or include a range of cool secret passages. I also feel that a murder mystery weekend is inherently interesting subject.

As a variation on this theme I considered a group of vampires hosted the event and slowly start eating people. I also considered having the supposed murder being an uninduced heart attack instead.

Strengths:

  • More light hearted than many settings.
  • Crime novels are very popular.
  • Potential for a lot of twists.
  • Could actually do the event/party game of the book.
  • Fairly low risk as in very few downsides.

Weakness

  • Not overly original and so relies heavily on writing quality.
  • Needs characters to be added to be a real concept as they are hugely important here.

Inspiration: Gosford Park, Agatha Christie, House on Haunted Hill and Dead Famous by Ben Elton.

Ideas book/Conversation.com

People love hearing concepts. Two people in a fairly dead end job discuss their myriad of ideas about all things.

I considered taking interesting concepts to an inherently interesting friend of mine, pitching them to him and recording the resulting conversation. The book could then be written based on these conversations with minor adjustments. Whilst doing this I also considered a website where interesting conversations are stored.

Strengths

  • You read this far, concepts must be interesting.
  • There are a lot of people with a lot of great ideas but no gumption or time to do them all gathering these ideas would be fun and probably quite easy.
  • Potentially easy to write due to the plan above.
  • Conversations allow you to change subject easily so the book can transition with your interests and therefore remain fun to write throughout.
  • The person who is always coming up with ideas but never does them is a believable and rarely used character with an obvious progression potential.

Weaknesses

  • Again very hard to categorise, it would go in general fiction where it would likely be more popular with science fiction or fantasy readers.
  • Could stagnate or become repetitive quite easily.

Inspiration Dragons Den, Clerks, High Fidelity.

Game of Thrones Tarot

In the Game of Thrones books, the political manoeuvrings of the council are to me the most interesting parts. In this book a powerful person in the council is an avid player of a card game which has some similarities to Tarot. The council therefore discuss their business whilst playing the game. A young and initially naive person joins the group purportedly because of his interest in the game and the seasoned councillors manipulate them whilst various stories of intrigue play out. Potentially the tarot like game itself could have some fortune telling or mystical component. This could also be a modern day book about poker played between high end powerbrokers.

Strength

  • An unusual approach to the fantasy genre.
  • Game of Thrones is low on similar works (Joe Abercrombie?) and has been vastly popular.
  • The game allows for a smaller setting of characters initially as fantasy books can be quite daunting in this regard.
  • A new player allows for fairly easy explanation.
  • This book is easy to twist multiple ways
  • If the game involves steep gambling and non-financial forfeits this can drive plot interestingly.
  • Gambling, fortune telling and intrigue are strong story telling devices.
  • Easy to categorise whilst also being original.

Weaknesses

  • Need to avoid the main character being too weak.
  • A extremely hard book to write.
  • There may be a glut of books copying Game of Thrones being written.

Inspiration: Game of Thrones, Rounders (the film not the ball game) and Tarot.

Darkfall

Meteors falls from the sky, slowly at first but rapidly increasing in pace, the meteors provide magic to a world that has long been without it. Initially welcomed, as the meteor showers increase and become more violent there is a distinct threat to the world. When Dark falls things change.

This has happened before, in times gone by a huge magical protective barrier was erected to keep the meteors out. The barrier is now failing but to recreate it, requires several of the world’s most powerful magicians and unsurprisingly they are not keen to volunteer; especially as the last group are believed to have died creating the barrier.

There is a lot left to do with this outline. You could probably merge it with the previous idea and construct an epic scale fantasy. It could be easily changed to a science fiction or modern day super hero theme. I used this story in my game Night, (called Night as both Dark Fall and Darkfall are taken) with a still not fully implemented but intended theme of a semi Wild West fantasy.

Initially following a cowboy hat wearing, goblin protagonist who, while not entirely nice, has been ousted from his lands repeatedly, by, both the encroachment of others and the general sentiment that you can do anything to goblins. The Goblin, despite the hat fills the Indians role and share some characteristics (maybe wigwams and war paints). The Goblin grows to become a powerful wizard. Meanwhile the book/books follows other wizards ascent to power in other, very different circumstances (I would not follow all in detail).

Onyx, pitch or villains heart, are black but yet the night,
Is darkest covering all within, its endless missing sight.
Evening brings forth darkness, as light exudes from open flame.
So with Cimmerian night, the tiny meteors share its name.

Strengths

  • Another somewhat new take on Fantasy but similar enough to remain familiar.
  • A lot of flexibility, I have numerous other ideas not mentioned here for this story line.
  • I very much like the idea of a group of disparate but powerful magicians trying to ally without backstabbing to allay the threat to the world. This is both ripe with allegorical potential but also with characters that have interesting emotional decisions toward the end.
  • I also like the potential of having more people able to complete the ritual than are needed and have the politicking around being left out of the potentially fatal ritual.

Weakness

  • Meteors are rather a clichéd fantasy trope.
  • Could be conflicted, there are child friendly elements but potentially very adult related content (if the meteors magic is drug like for example).
  • Making a goblin likeable and also interesting is mildly dangerous.
  • Could well be epic in scope and therefore take a very long time to write.

Inspiration: Final Fantasy X, the Wheel of Time, Star Dust, Firefly.
