Night is playable but the tutorials and interface especially, need work. Night is programmed in Action Script 3 (Flash).


Night aims to combine the Tower Defence and Collectable Card Game Genres. Tower Defence games are some of the most popular bowser games, unfortunately few have significant longevity as they often become repetitive in strategy. Cards offer gameplay impacting rewards for much lower cost/development time than alternatives meaning this issue can be solved.
