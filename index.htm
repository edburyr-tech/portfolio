Description of Work

I deliver effective training solutions to fit the needs of customers. This starts with requirements gathering, determining the most effective delivery method and considering the difference between learner’s ability and the desired competencies. If a computer based solution was agreed, a standard programming cycle begins. I develop the software, except for video production and extensive graphics which I outsource if required. I ensure a rapid build, a clean effective design, reinforcement of the key learning objectives and seamless deployment.

I can manage a LMS, build in a range of languages and programs, handle graphics manipulation, questions, learning games and audio. I make extensive use of templates, both mine and those created by others to ensure standardised look between linked packages and speed of build.
